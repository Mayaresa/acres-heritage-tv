Songs for Madam Vice President
A song gift for

Her Excellency Prof. Jane Naana Opoku-Agyemang

Vice President of the Republic of Ghana

Your Excellency,

Following our proposal, we offer you three songs from the Alliance for Change and Restoration (ACRES). Our alliance stands for the restoration of our farmlands and the reintroduction of our indigenous foods and seeds to improve the health of our people.

These songs carry the voices of Ghana's farmers, consumers, mothers and children, who long for clean food, healthy soil and the heritage foods our ancestors grew.

Please accept them, together with a bag of DIM Fonio (West Africa's oldest grain, from Ghana's pioneer fonio brand) and a pack of ACRES NANO Organic Fertilizer, as a small token of a big hope: that together we restore the land.

Nana Obeng-MayaresaFounder & PresidentAlliance for Change and Restoration(ACRES)
